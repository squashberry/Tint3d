Tint3d environment assets
==========================

The following authored 3D models and colour maps are from Kenney:

- Factory Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/factory-kit
- City Kit (Industrial): https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial
- Creator: Kenney / https://kenney.nl

These assets are public-domain dedications under CC0. Commercial use, modification,
and redistribution are permitted. Attribution is not required; credit is included
here as a courtesy.

Tint3d loads the GLB files locally from this repository. Each pack's
Textures/colormap.png file is kept next to its GLB models because the GLB files
reference that relative path.
